# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
SiliconValueIndex helps PC builders compare GPU price divided by benchmark FPS. Lower cost per FPS is better, subject to a playable FPS floor.
## Users
Inferred from the repository and request: PC builders choosing graphics cards by budget, resolution, rendering mode, and value.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Astro static site with overview chart, finder, rankings, comparisons, GPU details, methodology and source downloads. Preserve actual data, estimated-FPS labels, price dates, links and keyboard controls. No pipeline or scoring changes.
## Brand Commitments
The user delegated reference selection and implementation of a visually appealing Dribbble-inspired redesign.
## Evidence on Hand
README.md, data/site JSON, existing site/src routes. Prices are recorded snapshots, not live quotes.
